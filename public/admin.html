REAL-DEVICE VIVA TEST RIG (TEACHER VS FRIEND)

Live 2-Device Concurrency & Race Hazard Station

Test concurrency in real time across two separate devices (e.g. Teacher on Laptop and Friend on Mobile). Prime an event to 1 seat left, and demonstrate how toggling Mutex OFF causes both devices to claim the single seat (overselling to -1), while Mutex ON strictly confirms the first transaction and rejects the second with Sold Out.

🔴 TEST 1: Mutex OFF (Race Condition Occurs)
  1. In the table below, switch Event #1 Mutex to OFF.
  2. Click ⚡ Prime Event #1 to Exactly 1 Seat.
  3. Teacher & Friend both open Event #1 and click Book Team Pass.
  4. Both proceed without lock -> Both get confirmed passes!
  5. Remaining seats drops to -1 (OVERSOLD!)
🟢 TEST 2: Mutex ON (Mutual Exclusion Enforced)
  1. Switch Event #1 Mutex back to ON.
  2. Click ⚡ Prime Event #1 to Exactly 1 Seat.
  3. Teacher & Friend both click Book Team Pass.
  4. Whichever completes first secures the pass (seats -> 0).
  5. The 2nd device is serialized & rejected with "Sold Out"!
